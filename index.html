Remorse Chess

Sadists & Devastation

Chess, except the pieces have feelings about killing.

Every capture rolls a die. A piece might shrug it off, freeze with grief, refuse to fight again, or develop a taste for it. Bishops can absolve the troubled ones, if they get there in time. Play Pass & Play on one device, online through Game Center, or against the computer.

Common questions

How do I learn the rules?
Tap How to Play on the main menu. You can also tap any piece during a game to see its kills, its state and what that state means.

Online play says I'm not signed in.
Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings app, then come back to Remorse Chess.

I bought the unlock on another device.
Open the unlock screen (tap Medium or Hard under VS Computer) and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Is there a subscription or ads?
No. The game is free with one game at a time and the Easy computer. A single one-time purchase unlocks the rest.
