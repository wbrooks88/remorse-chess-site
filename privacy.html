Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Remorse Chess is a chess game for iPhone and iPad. It does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app saves your games in progress and a few settings (such as your preferred computer difficulty and which side you play) on your device only. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Online matches use Apple's Game Center. If you choose to sign in, Apple handles your Game Center identity and passes the moves of each turn-based match between players. We don't receive or store your Apple ID, email address or contacts. Your Game Center nickname is shown to the opponents you play. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy, and you can manage Game Center in your device's Settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether the unlock has been purchased on your Apple ID.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
